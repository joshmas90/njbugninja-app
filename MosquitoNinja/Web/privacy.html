PRIVACY.
SIMPLE AND CLEAR.

Appointments and reminders stay on your device. Location is used only when you choose to check the service area, and quote information is sent only when you choose Send.

App & website privacy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

  • Saved appointments are stored on your device for the appointment feature until you delete them or remove the app
  • 24-hour and 1-hour reminders are scheduled locally through iOS notifications
  • The app requests location only when you choose to check the service area
  • iOS location and geocoding services identify the county and ZIP code for that check
  • Mosquito Ninja does not receive or store your precise coordinates from the app location check
  • The app compares the result against public county and ZIP service-area rules
  • App quote details and optional property photos remain under your control until you choose Send in Apple’s Messages interface
  • When you send an app quote or text, your messaging service delivers the message and any selected photos to Mosquito Ninja; there is no separate Mosquito Ninja app account or app-side lead database
  • The public website quote form sends the contact and property details entered there to Mosquito Ninja’s company email inbox
  • The public website uses Plausible Analytics for aggregate, privacy-conscious website measurement; the native app does not use Plausible to track your in-app activity
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers may maintain standard server logs for security, reliability and operation of their services

Retention, deletion & privacy choices

Appointment information remains on your device and can be removed by deleting the appointment or removing the app. Mosquito Ninja does not maintain a server-side appointment account in this build.

Messages, quote requests, property details and photos that you choose to send to Mosquito Ninja may be retained in business email or messaging records as reasonably needed to respond, prepare or schedule service, provide customer support, maintain legitimate service or business records, prevent abuse, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations. Information is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer reasonably needed for those purposes, subject to records that must be retained by law or legitimate business requirements.

You may ask Mosquito Ninja to correct or delete personal information that Mosquito Ninja controls by emailing service@njbugninja.com. A request cannot remove information that must still be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or other legitimate recordkeeping obligations, and it does not control copies held independently by your carrier, email provider, Apple, hosting provider or another service you chose to use.
