OUTDOOR FLY CONTROL.
SOURCE-FOCUSED.

Targeted outdoor house-fly and nuisance-fly control for South Jersey properties. The discussion starts with where flies are breeding, feeding, resting and affecting the outdoor spaces people use.

Start with the source

Outdoor fly pressure is often tied to trash and recycling, pet waste, decaying organic material, persistent moisture, food-service areas or nearby breeding conditions. Source reduction and sanitation are central to the service discussion.

Where we look

  • Trash, recycling and dumpster surroundings
  • Pet areas and other organic material
  • Patios, decks, entrances and outdoor kitchens
  • Exterior resting surfaces and protected corners
  • Commercial outdoor-use and food-service areas

What to expect

Treatment, when appropriate, is limited to labeled sites and is selected for the actual pest and property conditions. Weather, sanitation, neighboring properties and ongoing source conditions can affect fly pressure, so complete elimination is not promised.

Tell Mosquito Ninja where the flies are worst and anything you have noticed about nearby waste, moisture, pet areas or food activity.
